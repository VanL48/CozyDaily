# CozyDaily
Link: https://vanl48.github.io/CozyDaily/CD_v1/index.html

CozyDaily is a personal productivity and lifestyle website designed to help users organize their daily routines, track habits, and reflect on their everyday lives.
Featuring a cozy and minimalist design, CozyDaily offers both Light Mode (White & Cream) and Dark Mode (Midnight Blue), allowing users to customize their experience.

**Built with:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

**Your space. Your pace.** 🤍
